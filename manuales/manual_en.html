📖 Prologue: The Great Internet Village

🏘️ The metaphor of letters and shops

Imagine that the Internet is a bustling village full of houses and shops:

  • Your Web Browser: You walking down the high street asking for things.
  • The Web Server: A shop attended by shopkeepers behind the counter.
  • An HTTP Request: A handwritten letter you send saying: "Please show me the toy catalog".
  • An HTTP Response: The parcel the shop returns containing what you asked for.

What is OpenSec Studio?

It is your magic detective backpack crafted by el saghet. Inside it, you carry tools to intercept letters mid-air (Interceptor), duplicate and send them a thousand times (Repeater), test key combinations (Intruder), check castle gates (DAK Scanner & Port Scanner), audit server configurations (SSH Host Audit), and consult a secret encyclopedia with over 1,200 threats even on airplane mode (Offline Feed).

🌟 Fundamental Principle: 100% Real, Zero Mock Data

In OpenSec Studio no button is a placeholder or mock simulation. Every single action executes native Rust code, communicates over live network sockets, and processes authentic packets.

Capítulo 3: The Customs Officer (HTTP Interceptor & HTTPS Tunnels)

The Interceptor sits between your browser and the remote server to inspect packets before transmission.

🛑 The customs inspector with a clipboard

Before a letter travels, the guard catches it mid-air. You can read it, edit contents with a pencil, and decide whether to Forward or Drop it.

Section Function in OpenSec Studio Metaphor to understand it
Intercept is ON / OFF Enables or pauses the mandatory packet inspection halt. Lowering or raising the tollgate barrier.
Forward (Proceed) Sends the edited letter onwards to the destination server. Waving the inspected vehicle through.
Drop (Discard) Terminates transmission and closes the socket connection. Confiscating and destroying suspect cargo.
🕵️‍♂️ Detective Mission 3: Edit a Message in Mid-Air

Enable interception, submit a form in your browser, alter a field value inside OpenSec, and click Forward.
